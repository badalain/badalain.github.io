    About this list

    This site shows publicly documented ownership links between everyday consumer brands and their parent companies, so people can make their own informed choices about what to buy. It does not tell you what to think about any organization — only who owns what, with a source for every claim.

    How an entry gets added

    1. A lead is found in a public source — a company website, a news report, or an aggregator.
    2. It is checked against a primary document: a company annual report's pattern of shareholding, a stock exchange filing, or a regulator's record.
    3. Only once confirmed against that primary document is it marked Verified, with the exact source and date shown.
    4. Ownership changes over time — stakes are sold, companies merge. Every entry is re-checked periodically, and the date it was last verified is always shown.

    What this site is not

    This site makes no claims beyond documented ownership. It does not host allegations, opinions, or user comments. Suggested alternatives are offered as options to consider, not endorsements.

    Corrections
