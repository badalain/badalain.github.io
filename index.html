    Install Badalain on your phone

    You don't have to install anything — this website works in any browser. Installing just puts an icon on your home screen and lets Badalain open with no internet connection. It is free and takes a few seconds.

    Your browser can install it right now:

    Uninstalled it and don't see an Install button any more? That's normal — your browser decides when to offer it. The steps below always work.

    Android — Chrome (most phones)

    1. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top right.
    2. Tap Install app. Some versions say Add to Home screen instead — then choose Install.
    3. Tap Install to confirm.

    Android — Samsung Internet

    1. Tap the menu button (three lines, bottom right).
    2. Tap Add page to, then Home screen.
    3. Tap Add.

    Android — Firefox

    1. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).
    2. Tap Install.
    3. Confirm when asked.

    Android — Opera or UC Browser

    The menu wording differs between versions. Open the browser's menu and look for Add to home screen (sometimes under Add to or Home screen). If you can't find it, open this same link in Chrome and follow the Chrome steps above. Opera Mini in data-saving mode can't keep the app for offline use, but the website still works whenever you are online.

    iPhone and iPad — Safari

    1. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add.

    Chrome, Firefox and Edge on iPhone don't always offer this. If you don't see Add to Home Screen in the Share menu, open this link in Safari instead.

    To remove it later

    Press and hold the Badalain icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall or Remove. You can install it again at any time with the steps above.

    About this list

    This site shows publicly documented ownership links between everyday consumer brands and their parent organizations, so people can make their own informed choices about what to buy. It does not tell you what to think about any organization — only who owns what, with a source for every claim.

    How an entry gets added

    1. A lead is found in a public source — a company website, a news report, or an aggregator.
    2. It is checked against a primary document: a company annual report's pattern of shareholding, a stock exchange filing, a credit-rating report, or an official record.
    3. Only once confirmed against that primary document is it marked Verified, with the exact source and date shown.
    4. Ownership changes over time — stakes are sold, companies merge. Entries are re-checked periodically, and the date each source was checked is always shown.

    About the alternatives

    Alternatives are ranked best first, based on market reputation, awards and consumer surveys. They are a lighter kind of research than the ownership facts and are offered as options to consider, not endorsements. Where a founder or leader has a military background, that is stated plainly.

    What this site is not

    This site makes no claims beyond documented ownership and what named sources have said on the record. It does not host allegations, opinions, or user comments.

    Corrections

    If an entry is inaccurate or out of date, every entry page has a "Report a problem" link, or use the Suggest / correct tab. Every submission is reviewed before anything changes.
